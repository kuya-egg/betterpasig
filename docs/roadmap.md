# BetterPasig.org: Roadmap at SMART Goals

Oct 19, 2026 · Louie Miguel
Live version: https://claude.ai/artifact/NZWvJbDKRjzhCLaBCJC8rm (this file is an export; the live doc wins if they differ)

## Buod

BetterPasig.org will be the one landing place where any Pasigueño can find a city service, benefit, opportunity or public record in under a minute, in Filipino or English. Pasig already publishes more than most cities. Our job is to make that information easy to find, easy to understand and easy to follow over time, while sending every transaction back to the city's official channels.

We launch before January 2027. By December 31, 2026 we commit to three headline targets:

- **Complete:** all 173 services in the city's Citizen's Charters published, each with office, fee, processing time, requirements and source.
- **Trusted:** all 30 barangays and every emergency number verified, then re-checked every 90 days.
- **Launched:** live to the public by December 15, with 8 in 10 residents in our tests finding what they need in under a minute.

## Bakit BetterPasig

Pasig is already one of the most open city governments in the country, so BetterPasig does not need to expose what is hidden. What it needs to do is organize what is already public and make it usable. Most civic tech projects act as watchdogs where records are closed. In Pasig the records are open but scattered, so we act as a guide and a tracker.

What Pasig already does:

- **Local FOI law since 2018.** [Ordinance No. 37, s. 2018](https://newsinfo.inquirer.net/1040986/pasig-passes-own-version-of-foi-bill/amp) presumes city records are open and gives the city 10 days to act on requests.
- **A dedicated transparency office.** Ugnayan sa Pasig handled 29,189 FOI requests and complaints from January to May 2024 alone.
- **Open procurement.** Biddings are livestreamed and observers can attend. The city reported ₱414.7M in savings in the first year of tighter bidding ([Rappler](https://www.rappler.com/philippines/247382-pasig-savings-after-tightened-public-biddings/)).
- **Recognition.** The City Council received DILG-NCR's 2025 Local Legislative Awards for Most Improved LGU and Exemplary Performance ([Manila Bulletin](https://mb.com.ph/2025/11/05/pasig-city-council-named-most-improved-local-government-by-dilg)).

Where residents still struggle:

| What the city publishes | Why it is hard to use | What BetterPasig adds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 31 separate Citizen's Charter PDFs, one per office | You must know which office to open; some files run hundreds of pages | One searchable page per service, in plain Filipino |
| Announcements on the PIO Facebook page | Payouts, job fairs and deadlines scroll away within days | A weekly "Ngayong linggo sa Pasig" list, kept and searchable |
| Bidding livestreams and BAC documents | Hours of video and scattered PDFs | A bids log: project, budget, winner, savings, link |
| Ordinances as yearly PDFs | No search, legal language | Searchable ordinances with one-line summaries |
| Hotlines on several pages, some conflicting | Unclear which number is current | One verified, dated list, re-checked every 90 days |

Our stance is to amplify the city's transparency, not compete with it. Every page links to the official source, and every transaction goes to the city's own channels.

## Vision, mission at mga prinsipyo

**Vision.** Walang Pasigueño na mapag-iiwanan dahil hindi niya alam ang serbisyo, benepisyo o oportunidad na para sa kanya. (No Pasigueño misses a service, benefit or opportunity just because they did not know it existed.)

**Mission.** Gather everything the City of Pasig offers and decides into one clear, sourced, bilingual place, and keep it current so residents can follow what is happening in their city.

**Principles**

1. **Source every claim.** Each fact links to the official document it came from, with the date we checked it.
2. **Point to the city, never replace it.** We explain and guide; payments, applications and complaints go through official channels.
3. **Non-partisan always.** No endorsements, no party colors, no political funding. Officials are listed equally.
4. **Built for every age.** Plain Filipino first, large text, works on a budget phone and slow data.
5. **Open by default.** Code and data are public on GitHub; anyone can suggest a correction.
6. **Admit mistakes fast.** Corrections are fixed within 48 hours and logged publicly.
7. **Collect nothing personal.** No accounts, no stored complaints, no tracking cookies.

## SMART goals

Seven goals cover the whole mission: complete information, accurate information, launch, opportunities, tracking, reach and community. Each has one number we can check and one deadline, and six of the seven land before January 2027.

| # | Goal | Measure | Target | Deadline | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Every city service is documented | Charter services published with office, fee, time, requirements and source | 173 of 173 | Nov 29, 2026 | Content lead |
| 2 | Every contact is verified | Hotlines and all 30 barangay pages confirmed by phone or official source, re-checked every 90 days | 100%, none older than 90 days | Nov 29, 2026 | Barangay stewards |
| 3 | Residents can use it on day one | Share of 30 tested residents, 10 of them seniors, who find office, fee and requirements in under 60 seconds | 80% before public launch | Dec 12, 2026 | Design lead |
| 4 | Opportunities reach people on time | Weekly list of payouts, scholarships, job fairs and deadlines, posted within 48 hours of the official announcement | First issue, then every week; 90% within 48 hours | Dec 1, 2026 | Content lead |
| 5 | Residents can follow what the city does | Bids log covering every BAC posting, plus a New City Hall page updated monthly | 100% of postings from November 2026 | Dec 15, 2026 | Data lead |
| 6 | People actually use it | Monthly unique visitors (cookieless analytics) | 5,000 in the first 30 days; 25,000 a month by mid-2027 | Jan 15, 2027 | Project lead |
| 7 | It runs on community, not one person | Barangay stewards (one per barangay) and volunteers with a merged contribution in the last 90 days | 15 stewards by year-end, all 30 by March 2027; 20 volunteers | Dec 31, 2026 | Project lead |

Guardrails that apply to every goal: corrections fixed within 48 hours, every page under 300 KB on first load, and hosting kept under ₱2,000 a year.

## Roadmap

We build in November and launch by December 15, 2026 with services, opportunities and tracking together. Public funds and deeper features follow in the first half of 2027, and the project becomes self-running before the 2028 election.

```
Oct 19 ── Nov 1 ─────── Nov 29 ── Dec 1 ── Dec 12 ── Dec 15 ── Dec 31 ──── Jun 2027 ──── 2028
 Prepare    Build              Soft launch   Test     PUBLIC    Measure    Deepen        Sustain
            (gate: data+       (gate: 173    (gate:   LAUNCH                (funds,       (neutral
             identity ready)    services)     80%)                           ordinances)   election guide)
```

**Now: Oct 19 to Nov 29, 2026 (prepare and build)**

- By Oct 25: contact the existing BetterPasig maintainer in the BetterLGU directory and agree on one shared project
- By Nov 1: Astro repo with the JSON data files and the new identity; every hotline verified by phone; meeting requested with Ugnayan sa Pasig and the PIO
- Nov 2 to 29: homepage and barangay map, 173 service pages, 30 barangay pages, officials directory, search in Filipino and English, weekly-list template
- Recruit at least 15 barangay stewards before launch

**Next: Dec 1 to 31, 2026 (launch)**

- Dec 1: soft launch to volunteers and friends; first weekly "Ngayong linggo sa Pasig" list
- Dec 1 to 12: test with 30 residents, 10 of them seniors, and fix what they trip on
- Dec 15: public launch with the launch video; bids log and New City Hall page live
- Rest of December: weekly lists, corrections within 48 hours, measure the first 30 days

**Later: 2027 to 2028 (deepen and sustain)**

- January to June 2027: budget explorer, audit summaries in plain Filipino, ordinance search, flood readiness, open data downloads
- July 2027 to 2028: a strictly neutral guide to the May 2028 election, steward handbook, funding partners and a handover plan

To make December, we cut the budget explorer, audit summaries, ordinance search and flood maps from the launch. They need slow PDF extraction and fact-checking, and rushing them risks publishing wrong numbers. The launch keeps everything residents use daily.

## Paano susukatin

We review these numbers monthly and revisit the roadmap every quarter. Baselines are zero until launch, except where our research already counted something.

| Metric | Baseline (Oct 2026) | How we measure | Checked |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Services documented | 173 found in charters, 0 published | Count of service pages with all fields and a source | Monthly |
| Fields still unconfirmed | Many entries marked "verify" in our data | Count of `verify: true` in the JSON files | Monthly |
| Contacts older than 90 days | 30 barangays not yet verified | Last-checked date on each barangay and hotline entry | Weekly |
| Monthly unique visitors | 0 | Cloudflare Web Analytics, no cookies | Monthly |
| Search success | Not yet tested | Moderated test with 30 residents, 10 of them seniors | Each phase |
| Searches with no result | 0 | Count of empty searches (terms only, no personal data) | Monthly |
| Opportunity posts on time | 0 | Hours between the official post and ours | Weekly |
| Correction turnaround | 0 | Median hours from report to fix in the public log | Monthly |
| Active volunteers | 1 | GitHub contributors and stewards active in 90 days | Quarterly |

Empty searches matter most: they show us which services people look for that we have not covered yet.

## Team, pamamahala at sustainability

A small core team runs the site, and a wider volunteer network keeps the information true. Roles can be shared until more people join.

| Role | Does | People needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Project lead | Sets priorities, owns goals 5 and 6, talks with the city and BetterGov | 1 |
| Tech lead | Astro site, JSON data, search, hosting, GitHub reviews | 1 to 2 |
| Content lead | Writes service pages and the weekly opportunities list in plain Filipino | 2 |
| Data lead | Bids log, budget and audit summaries, New City Hall tracker | 1 |
| Barangay stewards | Verify their barangay's contacts every 90 days | 30, one per barangay |
| Design and accessibility | Keeps the identity consistent, runs the resident tests | 1 |

**How we decide.** All changes go through GitHub pull requests that someone else reviews. Facts need a source before they merge. The non-partisan pledge and the corrections log are public.

**How we stay alive.** The site is static and hosted on Cloudflare's free tier, so costs stay near the domain fee of about ₱1,000 a year. We will apply for the BetterGov Fellowship, ask BetterGov for shared infrastructure, and recruit students from Pamantasan ng Lungsod ng Pasig and Rizal Technological University. We accept no money from candidates, parties or anyone doing business with the city.

**How we work with the city.** We request data through Ugnayan sa Pasig and eFOI, send the PIO a monthly list of broken links or outdated pages we find, and offer our clean data back for the city to reuse.

## Mga panganib at paano haharapin

The biggest risk is not technical: it is information going stale, or the site being read as political. Both are handled by process, not by one person's effort.

| Risk | What could happen | How we handle it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Information goes stale | A wrong fee or number sends a resident to the wrong place | Last-checked date on every page, 90-day re-checks, "verify" labels until confirmed, 48-hour corrections |
| Seen as partisan | Content is read as for or against an official, especially near the May 2028 election | Non-partisan pledge, equal listing of officials, no political funding, neutral wording reviewed by two people |
| The city sees us as competition | Officials ignore or push back on the site | Every page links to the official source; we share fixes and data back; we ask for a meeting with Ugnayan and the PIO early |
| Leadership change in 2028 | Mayor Sotto's third and final term ends June 30, 2028, and priorities may change | Build on records and processes, not on any official; keep our own copies of public documents with dates |
| Volunteer burnout | One or two people carry everything and stop | Small tasks anyone can take, barangay stewards, documented processes, quarterly check-ins |
| Duplicate efforts | The BetterLGU directory already lists a Pasig project in progress (maintainer @ericvelasco-dev) | Contact the maintainer first and offer to join forces before launch |
| Legal and privacy | Using the city seal, or storing personal data | Our own logo only; no accounts or stored complaints, in line with the Data Privacy Act |
| Official pages cannot be read automatically | pasigcity.gov.ph blocks automated readers, so updates are missed | Volunteers check those pages by hand; we request data through FOI |
