## Overrides of the skill's defaults

- Dark mode kept: the site already offers it and users rely on it.
- Real phone numbers and addresses: they are the product, from `data/hotlines.json` and `data/site.json`, each sourced.
- Self-hosted font: privacy, no Google Fonts.
- Lit panel ground replaced by `--pale`: the kit's sand/sky/sage grounds are off the city palette.
