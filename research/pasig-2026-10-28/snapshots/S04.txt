HEADS UP, PROPERTY OWNERS IN PASIG CITY!
October 3, 2026
Settle your 2027 Real Property Tax early! Pay your 2027 Real Property Tax in full before December 28, 2026 and enjoy a 15% discount!
Check the material for:
Photo #2: Schedule of payment of real property taxes for 2027 and the corresponding discounts*
Photo #3: Where and how you can pay (onsite and online options)
For those paying via online, access the online payment site through this link: https://onlinepayments.pasigcity.gov.ph/
Don’t miss this chance to pay early and avail of the discount!
For concerns related to online payments, you may email payments@pasigcity.gov.ph.
*applicable to properties without delinquencies only.
