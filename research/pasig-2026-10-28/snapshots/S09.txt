ABISO Pasig City Mega Parking I (Near Mutya ng Pasig Mega Market, Caruncho Ave., Brgy. San Nicolas)
October 6, 2026
Pansamantalang isasara ang 3rd Floor ng Mega Parking I bukas, Miyerkules, October 7, 2026, mula 08:00AM hanggang 12:00NN.
Ito ay upang bigyang-daan ang REPAIR AT MAINTENANCE WORKS (flushing, garbage hauling, electrical system repair, repainting, atbp), bilang bahagi ng pagpapanatili ng kalinisan at maayos na kondisyon ng pasilidad.
Samantala, mananatili namang bukas ang 1st Floor, 2nd Floor, at 4th Floor ng Mega Parking I.
Muling bubuksan ang 3rd Floor nito simula 12:01PM ng parehong araw, October 7, 2026.
Maraming salamat sa inyong pang-unawa.
