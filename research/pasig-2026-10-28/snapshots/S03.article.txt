[UPDATED AS OF OCTOBER 4, 2026] 4th Quarter Assessment and Payment of Business Taxes and Fees
October 4, 2026
Please be reminded that the assessment and payment for the 4th quarter business taxes and fees is scheduled from October 1-31*, 2026. Failure to pay within the prescribed deadline will result in the imposition of penalties.
Refer to the material for the list of onsite locations where you may process your business tax payments. Regardless of the barangay where your business is located in Pasig City, you may pay at any onsite payment location. Visit the one nearest or most convenient to you. Please refer to the material for the updated operating schedule of each payment location.
For those who cannot pay onsite, payments may be processed through the Pasig City Online Business Permit Renewal and Payment System. Just visit, register, and pay through: https://onlinepayments.pasigcity.gov.ph.
Online transactions will only be accepted until 05:00PM on October 28, 2026 (Wednesday) to allow the Business Permit and Licensing Department sufficient time to process, reconcile, and validate all transactions before the renewal period ends.
Please be guided accordingly.
*The deadline for the fourth quarter payment is until October 31, 2026. If this will be declared as a special (non-working) holiday, the next working day, November 3, 2026, shall be the payment deadline.
—
ABISO
Ipinapabatid ng Business Permit and Licensing Department (BPLD) sa lahat ng business taxpayers sa Lungsod ng Pasig ang schedule ng pagtanggap ng mga transaksyon para sa Fourth Quarter Business Tax Payment mula October 1-31*, 2026.
I-check ang post para sa transactions sites. Bukod sa onsite payment, maaari ring magbayad online sa pamamagitan ng Pasig City Online Business Permit Renewal and Payment System.
Pumunta sa pinakamalapit o pinakaconvenient na transaction site. Mangyaring i-check ang material para sa updated na operating schedule ng bawat payment location.
Upang magkaroon ng sapat na oras ang lokal na pamahalaan para sa payment validation, posting, at reconciliation bago ang itinakdang deadline, tatanggap lamang ang online system ng mga transaksyon hanggang 05:00PM ng October 28, 2026 (Miyerkules).
Para sa mga katanungan o iba pang concern, maaaring makipag-ugnayan sa tanggapan ng BPLD.
Maraming salamat po.
*Ang deadline para sa fourth quarter payment ay hanggang October 31, 2026. Kung ito ay idedeklara bilang isang special (non-working) holiday, ang susunod na working day, November 3, 2026, ang ituturing na payment deadline.
